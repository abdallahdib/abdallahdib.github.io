Abdallah Dib

I am a research scientist working at Ubisoft La Forge (Montreal). Previously, I worked as a Senior Research Scientist at InterDigital R&I (France) and Technicolor Research, and as a Research Engineer at Inria. I obtained my PhD from Inria in 2016.

I develop methods for face capture, facial animation, and avatar reconstruction for video games and interactive entertainment My passion lies in conducting applied research that tackles real-world production challenges—from identifying workflow bottlenecks to developing novel techniques that become integrated tools, enabling more efficient workflows for artists and creators.

Research Interests: Computer Vision, Computer Graphics, Machine Learning, Generative Models, Neural Rendering, Self-Supervised Learning, Disentangled representations

Contact: deeb.abdallah [at] gmail [dot] com

I supervise research interns and PhD students, and I am always seeking motivated candidates to contribute to state-of-the-art projects.

Selected Projects

A blog post published on Ubisoft website, showcasing our work on MoSAR, a novel technique that transforms a single portrait photo into a fully relightable and detailed 3D avatar, streamlining character creation workflows for artists.
A technical paper presents FaceLab, our facial performance capture solution that enabled MPC artists to automatically extract facial performances from single camera footage. This technology successfully processed over 260 shots for the 2019 film Cats.
NextFace, my open-source PyTorch library for high-fidelity 3D face reconstruction from monocular RGB image. NextFace estimates facial attributes including 3D geometry, PBR materials (diffuse, specular, roughness), camera parameters, and illumination. With over 770 stars and 100 forks, NextFace serves as a state-of-the-art baseline for the computer vision research community.
We release FFHQ-UV-Intrinsics, a unique dataset containing intrinsic maps for 10,000 subjects including: diffuse, specular, ambient occlusion, translucency, and normal maps. The dataset is Available publicly for research use from here.
